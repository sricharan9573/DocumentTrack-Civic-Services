- Users can manually update the status in our application after checking the official government portal.

2. PRODUCT CONCEPT
==================================================

DocumentTrack helps citizens manage the complete journey of government applications.

The platform provides:

1. Government service discovery
2. Service information
3. Eligibility information
4. Required document checklist
5. Application fee information
6. Expected processing time
7. Official government portal link
8. Application tracking
9. Application reference number storage
10. Application status timeline
11. Manual status updates
12. Application reminders/important dates
13. Personal dashboard
14. Search and filtering
15. Profile/settings

The platform DOES NOT process government applications itself.

Correct user journey:

Citizen
   ↓
DocumentTrack
   ↓
Find Government Service
   ↓
View requirements
   ↓
Click "Apply on Official Portal"
   ↓
Official Government Website
   ↓
Citizen submits application
   ↓
Citizen receives Application/Reference Number
   ↓
Citizen returns to DocumentTrack
   ↓
Adds Application Number
   ↓
Tracks application from DocumentTrack
   ↓
Clicks "Track on Official Portal" whenever they want to verify the real government status
   ↓
Manually updates status in DocumentTrack
